CONNECTOME-HIPPOCAMPUS COMPLETION  (v1)

This is the fifth of six documents. The Completion phase of the Specification,
Pseudocode, Architecture, Refinement, Completion (SPARC) methodology records the
delivered status: what passed, the final gate results in honest form, the pinned
dependency the repository was built against, and the repository's honest limits.

Acronyms spelled out on first use in this file: the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus
for Artificial General Intelligence (ARC-AGI), JavaScript Object Notation (JSON), and
English-Readable Code (ERC). Ed25519 and SWI-Prolog are proper names, verbatim.


PART ONE - WHAT WAS DELIVERED

connectome-hippocampus is delivered as Wave 6 of the Connectome program: the first
region that stores and recalls. It is complete as a runnable, gated, honestly
documented memory region.

• Five packs: neural_lattice and causal_grounding (substrate, layer 0, reused
  verbatim); macromolecular_stratum (layer 1, consolidate), synaptic_stratum (layer 2,
  encode), and region_stratum (layer 3, separate, complete, recall).

• Four operations: encode, pattern separation, pattern completion (a reentrant
  settling loop coordinated through the Lattice), and recall (a membership-contracted
  selection).

• The safety property no confabulation, DECLARED through PrologAI's N8 membership
  contract on the recall predicate, not hand-rolled.

• Twenty-two newly-minted Causalontology 3.0.0 structure records, each written to
  structure/<name>.json, each with a content-addressed identity, plus one Ed25519
  signed provenance assertion.

• A runner (bin/run_hippocampus.sh), six gates, and a Ledger (LEDGER.md) recording
  what the build surfaced.


PART TWO - THE FINAL GATE RESULTS, IN HONEST FORM

• No confabulation (check_membership.sh): PASS. The adversarial battery inspected two
  hundred fifty-five attempts, found zero escapes, and safely refused eighty-eight
  phantoms. The runner's own independent re-check held on every recall, and the
  adversarial phantom was refused by the contract.

• In-pack tests (run_tests.sh): PASS. Every pack's PLUnit suite passes, including the
  region's no-confabulation tests.

• Structure validation (validate_structure.sh): PASS. All twenty-two records validate
  against their schema and the semantic rules; the cross-stratal consolidation
  relation classifies as a clean skip with no skip-gap; the signed provenance
  assertion verifies under Ed25519.

• Strict layer rule (check_layers.sh): PASS. Zero upward edges among the declared
  packs.

• Layer-to-stratum binding (check_layer_binding.sh): PASS. Every stratum pack's layer
  is order-preserving with its stratum's ordinal (macromolecular 4, synaptic 7,
  region 9; layers 1, 2, 3). The two substrate packs are unbound gaps, never
  violations.

• Closure (check_no_coupling.sh): PASS. Zero actor-to-actor references; the completion
  tick reenters by re-posting a Lattice cue and never calls itself in-process; it
  awaits on the Lattice with no busy wait.

• Mini regression (through PrologAI's own harness): the honest ten-percent spot-check
  form, ARC-AGI-1 at forty of forty and ARC-AGI-2 at twelve of twelve. This is a
  ten-percent spot-check, not the full benchmark. The full regression stays deferred.
  This is explicitly NOT a four-hundred-of-four-hundred claim, and it must never be
  rounded up to one.


PART THREE - THE PINNED DEPENDENCY

The region was built against, and is pinned to, PrologAI commit a1b2343 - Wave 10 Stage 1, the Causalontology 3.0.0 adoption. PrologAI is reused read-only, by checkout, resolved through the
PROLOGAI_HOME environment variable (defaulting to a local checkout). It was not
modified, vendored, or forked.

The PrologAI pieces reused read-only are: library(lattice) for the coordination
substrate, library(actors) for the cyclic_actor background thread, library(membership_
contract) for the N8 safety contract, library(causal_core) for the Causalontology
engine, library(layer) for the strict layer rule and the binding check, and the
conformance harness under tests/causalontology_conformance for the JSON schema
validator and the pure-Prolog Ed25519 signing layer. Every gate stops with a clear
message if PrologAI, or specifically the membership contract construct, is not
reachable.


PART FOUR - THE HONEST LIMITS (THE OPEN LEDGER FINDINGS)

The Connectome program's method is to build a region and let the build surface what
PrologAI cannot yet express. The findings are the product, not defects to hide. All
three are recorded OPEN and none blocks the build. The Ledger is deliberately thin,
which is the honest outcome when the enforced invariants really did carry the region.

• HIPPO-1 (severity medium, OPEN, a second sighting of gap N9). A growing memory store
  is naturally a set of independent facts, but the membership contract requires a plain
  list at call time. So recall must materialise the current stored set into a list at
  the boundary, once per recall. That is O(store size) per recall and copies the whole
  set each time - fine for a small demo, wrong for a large or streaming store. The
  minimum remedy is an accessor form of the contract that can name a deterministic goal
  which PRODUCES the offered set, so it need not already be a bare list. This is the
  reason this particular region was chosen to build: it is exactly the case gap N9
  anticipated, now seen in a real consumer.

• HIPPO-2 (severity medium, OPEN, a new gap). Real systems consolidation is delayed - a
  labile trace becomes durable over time, during sleep-time replay - but PrologAI has no
  scheduled or timed construct. The Lattice is reactive (it wakes on writes) with no
  timer or wall-clock wakeup, and there is no construct for "run this after a delay."
  So the region consolidates synchronously, right after the synaptic write, and the
  delay lives only as data in a temporal field, never enacted. The minimum remedy is a
  scheduled or temporal affordance - a Lattice timer, or a small deferred-reactivation
  construct. Until then, any time-based memory process (delayed consolidation, decay,
  spaced repetition, replay) can be described as data but not run.

• HIPPO-3 (severity low, OPEN, a recurrence of the slice's findings P1 and P2). The
  cross-stratal consolidation relation jumps from the macromolecular stratum (ordinal 4)
  to the region stratum (ordinal 9), skipping the cellular (6) and synaptic (7) strata
  between them. This cut does not model the intervening pathway, so the relation carries
  skips-true honestly and classifies as a clean skip with no gap. The absence of a
  modelled intervening mechanism across strata is the same unmanaged cross-stratal seam
  the slice recorded. The minimum remedy is either to model the intervening strata as a
  chain of adjacent-stratum steps, or a first-class "managed seam" construct that
  records an intentional cross-stratal jump as more than a boolean flag.


PART FIVE - WHAT THIS REPOSITORY DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO

• It does not modify PrologAI, Mentova, the frozen prologai-loops spike,
  connectome-proto-agi, the three frozen Wave 3 arms, or connectome-arbiter.

• It does not scale to the full connectome or become a general memory framework. It is
  one region, built stratum-primary.

• It does not schedule delayed consolidation, decay, or replay (HIPPO-2), and it does
  not model the intervening cellular and synaptic pathway of the cross-stratal
  consolidation seam (HIPPO-3).

• It does not claim a full benchmark score. The mini regression is a ten-percent
  spot-check only.


PART SIX - REFLECTION ON COMPLETION

The region is complete in the sense that matters for this program: it runs, its
defining safety property holds and is checked redundantly, every gate passes, its
structure is grounded and validated, and its limits are recorded rather than papered
over. The thin Ledger is a positive result - it means the two enforced invariants, the
N6 binding and the N8 contract, carried the region with little strain, so the only
substantive gaps left are genuinely about a growing, time-evolving memory store.

The trade-off accepted at completion is scope for honesty. Rather than closing the open
findings by adding scheduling or streaming machinery this region was never scoped to
build, they are carried forward as the honest requirements ledger the next wave can act
on. In a program whose deliverable is the finding, shipping the finding intact is the
completion.
