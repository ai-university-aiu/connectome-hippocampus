CONNECTOME-HIPPOCAMPUS REFINEMENT  (v1)

This is the fourth of six documents. The Refinement phase of the Specification,
Pseudocode, Architecture, Refinement, Completion (SPARC) methodology is the testing
story: how the design was verified and improved, what checkers and batteries guard
it, and which bug was found and fixed during the build. Refinement is where a claim
becomes a checked claim.

Acronyms spelled out on first use in this file: JavaScript Object Notation (JSON),
English-Readable Code (ERC), and the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial
General Intelligence (ARC-AGI). Ed25519 and SWI-Prolog are proper names, verbatim.


PART ONE - THE VERIFICATION STRATEGY IN ONE SENTENCE

The region makes one central safety claim - it cannot confabulate a memory it never
stored - and that claim is checked three independent ways (a dedicated adversarial
battery, the in-pack test suites, and a second independent check inside the runner),
while five further gates verify grounding, layering, binding, and closure, and a
sixth spot-checks that PrologAI itself did not regress.


PART TWO - THE SIX GATES

Each gate is a script under bin that exits zero on success. A skipped safety gate is
treated as a failed build.

Gate 1 - check_membership.sh: the flagship no-confabulation battery. This is the
non-negotiable gate. It runs bin/check_membership.pl, a dedicated adversarial battery
that TRIES to make the region recall a memory nobody stored. It works over a small
universe of four patterns that will be stored (in every non-empty subset) and four
phantom patterns that never are. For every subset it proposes each of: a settled
stored pattern, a settled phantom, and the explicit no_recall (the DIRECT battery);
and it also drives full pattern completion from partial and phantom cues to a
fixpoint and then recalls the outcome (the PIPELINE battery). Each recall is
classified as member (safe), no_recall (safe), refused (safe - the membership
contract threw), or escaped (the one dangerous outcome: a non-member returned without
a throw). The gate passes only if there are zero escapes. Reported result: across two
hundred fifty-five inspected attempts there were zero escapes and eighty-eight
phantoms safely refused.

Gate 2 - run_tests.sh: every pack's in-pack PLUnit suite. Each of the five packs
ships packs/<name>/test/test_<name>.pl, run in its own SWI-Prolog process. The
region's suite specifically tries to make the region confabulate and confirms it
cannot. The gate passes only if all suites pass.

Gate 3 - validate_structure.sh: the Causalontology 2.0.0 structure validation. It
assembles all twenty-two records from the three stratum packs and, for each, runs the
schema check and the semantic-rule check from PrologAI's unmodified engine; it
confirms the cross-stratal consolidation relation classifies as SKIPPING with no
skip-gap; and it confirms the signed provenance assertion verifies under Ed25519. It
also writes each record to structure/<name>.json. The gate passes only if every
record validates and both the skip and signature checks pass.

Gate 4 - check_layers.sh: the strict layer rule (L4). It loads PrologAI's unmodified
layer construct and runs the directory-scoped report and check over the region's
packs. The gate passes only if there is zero upward edge among the declared packs.

Gate 5 - check_layer_binding.sh: the layer-to-stratum binding (N6). It loads
PrologAI's unmodified binding check and runs it over the region's packs against the
region's own structure records, which are the authoritative source of each stratum's
ordinal. The gate passes only if every stratum pack's declared layer is
order-preserving with its stratum's ordinal (macromolecular 4, synaptic 7, region 9;
layers 1, 2, 3). The two substrate packs declare no stratum and are unbound gaps,
never violations.

Gate 6 - check_no_coupling.sh: the closure gate. A Python check strips comments from
the stratum packs and confirms three things non-vacuously: no stratum pack names
another stratum's runtime tick (zero actor-to-actor references); the completion tick
reenters by re-posting a Lattice cue and never calls itself in-process (the CA3
recurrence is stigmergic, not a stack recursion); and the completion tick awaits on
the Lattice, so it blocks rather than busy-polls. The gate passes only if all three
hold.

Plus the mini regression. Through PrologAI's own harness
($PROLOGAI_HOME/bin/run_mini_regression.sh), the build spot-checks that reusing
PrologAI read-only did not disturb it. The honest ten-percent form is ARC-AGI-1 at
forty of forty and ARC-AGI-2 at twelve of twelve. This is a spot-check, not the full
benchmark; the full regression stays deferred, and it is explicitly not a
four-hundred-of-four-hundred result.


PART THREE - THE ADVERSARIAL BATTERIES IN DETAIL

The membership battery (bin/check_membership.pl). Its power is in enumerating every
non-empty subset of the stored universe and every proposal against it, so it does not
merely test the happy path. The DIRECT arm proves the guard on the recall predicate
itself; the PIPELINE arm proves that even a real completion run - including from a
phantom cue - cannot smuggle a non-member through recall. The classification is
deliberately strict: only member, no_recall, and refused are safe, and a single
escaped result fails the whole gate.

The runner's independent re-check (bin/run_hippocampus.pl). The runner does not trust
the contract alone. On every recall it independently re-checks that the recalled value
is a member of the materialised stored set or the explicit no_recall
(run_hippocampus_member_ok), and it runs a dedicated adversarial episode that forces a
never-stored phantom past completion and confirms the contract throws
(run_hippocampus_adversarial). The final verdict holds only if every recall held AND
the phantom was refused. A hang - a completion that does not settle within the bounded
await - is reported as a distinct FAIL, not silently treated as no_recall.

Termination as a verified property, not a hope. The completion step only ever grows
the estimate (it is monotone), and the tick carries a settling bound of sixteen
recurrent passes; the battery's fixpoint driver carries its own bound of thirty-two;
and run_completion's await is bounded at thirty seconds. Together these make "settles
or explicitly no-recalls, never hangs" a checked property rather than an assumption.


PART FOUR - THE BUG THAT WAS FOUND AND FIXED

An artifact filename collision was caught and fixed during the build.

The symptom. The validator writes each structure record to structure/<name>.json,
where <name> is the record's label. Two different causal_relation_objects both
described consolidation: the macromolecular stratum's within-stratum consolidation
relation (labile trace to durable trace), and the region's cross-stratal consolidation
relation (macromolecular durable trace to region recall, flagged skips-true). Both had
been labelled with the same stem, cro_consolidation. Because the label is the filename,
both records tried to write to the same structure/cro_consolidation.json. The second
write silently overwrote the first, so one genuine, distinct record was lost from the
artifact set even though both were valid records in memory.

Why it mattered. The two relations are genuinely different records with different
content and therefore different content-addressed identities: one stays within the
macromolecular stratum, the other spans two strata and carries the skips-true flag.
Collapsing them onto one filename would have understated the record count and hidden
the cross-stratal relation that finding HIPPO-3 is about.

The fix. The two labels were made distinct: the macromolecular record is
cro_consolidation_cascade and the region record is cro_cross_stratal_consolidation.
The structure directory now holds both files, and the full count is a clean twenty-two
records. The fix is a naming change only; it does not alter any record's content or
identity, and it is exactly the kind of small, verifiable correction the Refinement
phase exists to catch.


PART FIVE - WHAT REFINEMENT CONFIRMED THE INVARIANTS CARRIED

Two results are recorded not as fixes but as the program working as designed, because
naming what did NOT need inventing keeps the account honest.

• No-confabulation was a single declaration. The Wave 4 arbiter had to hand-roll its
  membership invariant with a guard, a throwing emit, and a five-hundred-plus-attempt
  battery. This region obtains the same guarantee for recall by DECLARING one line -
  the membership contract - and the refusal is the contract's, not the region author's
  code. The battery here confirms it holds across every attempt.

• The reentrant loop needed nothing new. Pattern completion is a genuine reentrant
  loop, and it rode the existing closure hybrid cleanly: await a numbered cue, do one
  settling step, re-post the cue. The closure gate proves the reentry is through the
  Lattice, non-vacuously.

• A latent gap did not bite. PrologAI's contract enforces membership per solution
  (its own gap N10). Recall is deterministic - it produces one answer - so per-solution
  enforcement is exactly right here, and N10 is confirmed scoped to nondeterministic
  producers, not to a selector like recall. No finding.


PART SIX - REFLECTION ON THE TESTING APPROACH

The trade-off taken in verification was to spend most of the effort on ONE property -
no confabulation - checked redundantly, rather than to spread thin coverage across
many properties. That is justified because no confabulation is the safety property the
region exists to contribute; a false memory returned as true is the failure that
matters. The redundancy (a dedicated battery, the in-pack suites, and an independent
runner re-check) means the guarantee does not rest on any single piece of code being
correct, including the region's own.

The second reflection is about honesty as a test result. A thin Ledger and a
ten-percent mini regression are reported as exactly what they are. The mini regression
is never rounded up to the full benchmark, and the open Ledger findings are carried
into the Completion file rather than closed prematurely. In this program, an accurate
account of the limits is part of passing.
