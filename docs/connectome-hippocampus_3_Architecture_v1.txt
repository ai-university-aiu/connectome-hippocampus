CONNECTOME-HIPPOCAMPUS ARCHITECTURE  (v1)

This is the third of six documents. The Architecture phase of the Specification,
Pseudocode, Architecture, Refinement, Completion (SPARC) methodology designs the
skeleton of the system: its major components, the boundaries between them, the
substrate they run on, and how they are wired. This file describes the structure of
connectome-hippocampus exactly as it is built.

Acronyms and proper names used here, spelled out on first use in this file:
English-Readable Code (ERC), JavaScript Object Notation (JSON), long-term
potentiation (LTP), and the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General
Intelligence (ARC-AGI). Ed25519 and SWI-Prolog are proper names, kept verbatim.


PART ONE - THE ORGANIZING PRINCIPLE: STRATUM-PRIMARY

The region is decomposed one pack per biological stratum on the OUTSIDE, with the
individual constructs living as independently testable sub-modules on the INSIDE.
This is the Wave 3 verdict of the Connectome program, and it is the same shape the
Wave 4 arbiter used. A "stratum" here is a level of biological organization named by
Causalontology 2.0.0 and given a numeric ordinal; a lower ordinal is a finer level.

Two supporting packs sit beneath the strata as substrate: a coordination Lattice and
a shared minting vocabulary. They belong to no stratum and are reused verbatim from
earlier Connectome work.


PART TWO - THE FIVE PACKS, THEIR LAYERS AND STRATA

Each pack declares a numeric layer (used by the strict layer rule) and, if it sits at
a stratum, a named stratum (used by the layer-to-stratum binding). The layer numbers
are internal to this repository; the stratum ordinals are read from the structure
records.

Pack: neural_lattice
    Layer 0. No stratum (substrate, unbound).
    Role: the coordination Lattice - stigmergy for state plus notification for
    reactivity. Regions coordinate only by posting and awaiting numbered phase cues;
    they never address one another. Reused verbatim from the Wave 2 slice.
    Public predicates include neural_lattice_open, neural_lattice_reset,
    neural_lattice_post_cue, neural_lattice_await_cue, neural_lattice_hop,
    neural_lattice_narrate, and the retained hop trace.

Pack: causal_grounding
    Layer 0. No stratum (substrate, unbound).
    Role: the shared Causalontology 2.0.0 minting vocabulary - fourteen helpers that
    stamp a content-addressed identity, fold key-value fields, build id-keyed maps,
    derive a deterministic signing key, and sign an assertion. Reused verbatim so a
    record minted here has byte-identical content, and therefore a byte-identical
    identity, to the same record elsewhere. Public predicates include cm_stratum,
    cm_cnt, cm_occ, cm_rlz, cm_port, cm_conduit, cm_cro, cm_map_of, and
    cm_signed_assertion_over.

Pack: macromolecular_stratum
    Layer 1. Stratum: macromolecular (ordinal 4).
    Role: CONSOLIDATE - the calcium-driven molecular cascade that raises a labile
    trace toward durable. The dynamics are kept native as a rate law, never dressed
    as a causal_relation_object. The pack is PURE: no runtime tick, no Lattice
    coordination; it is invoked downward during encoding. It mints six structure
    records. Public predicates: macromolecular_stratum_consolidate,
    macromolecular_stratum_is_durable, macromolecular_stratum_durable_occurrent,
    macromolecular_stratum_stratum, macromolecular_stratum_records.

Pack: synaptic_stratum
    Layer 2. Stratum: synaptic (ordinal 7).
    Role: ENCODE - long-term potentiation writes an input pattern as a labile memory
    trace and sets its initial strength. The pattern is written unchanged, so the set
    of stored patterns is exactly the set of encoded inputs. The dynamics are native.
    The pack is PURE: no runtime tick. It mints four structure records. Public
    predicates: synaptic_stratum_encode, synaptic_stratum_potentiate,
    synaptic_stratum_potentiation_occurrent, synaptic_stratum_stratum,
    synaptic_stratum_records.

Pack: region_stratum
    Layer 3. Stratum: region (ordinal 9).
    Role: the hippocampal circuit - SEPARATE (dentate gyrus), COMPLETE (CA3, the
    reentrant loop), and RECALL (the membership-contracted selection). This is the
    only pack with a runtime actor (the completion tick). It mints twelve structure
    records and declares the membership contract on recall. Public predicates include
    region_stratum_separate, region_stratum_best_match, region_stratum_complete_step,
    region_stratum_complete_tick, region_stratum_run_completion, region_stratum_recall,
    region_stratum_store_encode, region_stratum_store_patterns,
    region_stratum_encode_memory, and the structure accessors.

The cellular stratum (ordinal 6) is deliberately not occupied: no construct in this
cut of the model lives there. That absence is honest and is why the cross-stratal
consolidation relation (macromolecular ordinal 4 to region ordinal 9) is flagged as
a clean skip.


PART THREE - THE PACK GRAPH (a text diagram)

Arrows point in the direction of a dependency (a pack imports the pack it points to).
Every edge goes downward or sideways; there is no upward edge.

    region_stratum            (layer 3, stratum region, ordinal 9)
       |   \        \
       |    \        \-------------------------\
       v     v                                  v
    synaptic_stratum      macromolecular_stratum      (layers 2 and 1)
       |     (ordinal 7)      |   (ordinal 4)
       |                      |
       v                      v
    causal_grounding   <---   (both strata import the minting vocabulary)
                              (layer 0, substrate)

    region_stratum  --->  neural_lattice   (layer 0, substrate: the memory store,
                                            the reentrant loop, the narration)

    region_stratum  --->  [PrologAI, read-only]:
                              library(membership_contract)  (N8, the recall contract)
                              library(causal_core)          (the classify engine)
                              library(lattice)              (the raw Lattice door)

Reading the graph. The region imports the two strata below it (downward, for the
encode pipeline and the cross-stratal grounding), the Lattice substrate (for the
store and the reentrant loop), and the minting vocabulary. The two strata are pure
and import only the minting vocabulary. Nothing imports upward. This is what the
strict layer gate verifies.


PART FOUR - THE SUBSTRATE AND THE LATTICE COORDINATION

There is exactly one coordination nexus, opened by neural_lattice_open, addressed by
a fixed locus URI. Coordination is by numbered PHASE CUES, never by one component
naming another:

• Phase 1 carries the settle cue: an estimate, the materialised stored patterns, a
  pass counter, and a token. The completion tick awaits phase 1, does one settling
  step, and either re-posts phase 1 (reentry) or posts phase 2.

• Phase 2 carries the settled result: settled(Pattern) or no_recall. The driver's
  run_completion awaits phase 2, bounded, and reads the result.

The await is a genuine block on a private message queue, woken by the notification
that every Lattice write performs. There is no busy wait. Because exactly one phase
cue exists at any instant, the loop is deterministic and the glass-box narration
reads as a clean sequence.

The MEMORY STORE also lives on the Lattice: encoding deposits one stored fact per
memory (stigmergy), and store_patterns reads them back. This is why recall must
materialise the growing set into a list at the boundary - the architectural seam that
the Ledger records as finding HIPPO-1.


PART FIVE - THE INTERFACES BETWEEN PACKS

• Encode pipeline (region to synaptic to macromolecular). region_stratum_encode_memory
  calls synaptic_stratum_encode to write a labile trace, deposits it into the store,
  then calls macromolecular_stratum_consolidate to raise it toward durable. These are
  downward structural calls, permitted reuse, not runtime tick coupling.

• Structure grounding (region to both strata). The region's cross-stratal
  consolidation causal_relation_object names the macromolecular durable-trace
  occurrent as its cause and the region recall occurrent as its effect; its cue-input
  port names the synaptic potentiation occurrent. These are references to records the
  lower packs mint, not runtime calls.

• The completion loop (region to itself, through the Lattice). The tick reenters only
  by re-posting a phase cue. There is no in-process recursion and no reference from
  one stratum's tick to another's - the closure gate proves this non-vacuously.

• The safety interface (region to PrologAI). Recall declares the membership contract;
  PrologAI's N8 construct wraps recall and refuses any non-member at runtime.


PART SIX - HOW PROLOGAI IS REUSED, READ-ONLY

PrologAI is reused by checkout, never vendored or forked, and never edited. Every
gate resolves PrologAI through the PROLOGAI_HOME environment variable, defaulting to
a local checkout, and stops with a clear message if PrologAI - or specifically the
membership contract construct - is not reachable. The region is pinned to PrologAI
commit 2043a09, the Wave 5 membership contract tip.

The PrologAI packs and harnesses reused, all read-only, are:

• library(lattice) - the raw stigmergy plus await and notify door.
• library(actors), providing cyclic_actor - the background thread the completion
  tick runs on.
• library(membership_contract) - the N8 construct recall declares (the safety core).
• library(causal_core) - the Causalontology 2.0.0 engine: content-addressed
  identity, kinds, semantic rules, and the cross-stratal classify and skip-gap
  algorithms.
• library(layer) - the L4 strict layer rule and the N6 layer-to-stratum binding,
  run unmodified over this region's packs.
• The conformance harness under tests/causalontology_conformance, providing the JSON
  schema validator (schema_check) and the pure-Prolog Ed25519 signing layer.


PART SEVEN - THE STRUCTURE RECORDS (the grounded skeleton)

The region mints twenty-two Causalontology 2.0.0 records, distributed by stratum:

• macromolecular_stratum (six): the stratum, the calcium/calmodulin-kinase cascade
  bearer, the labile- and durable-trace occurrents, the consolidation disposition,
  and the consolidation cascade causal_relation_object (labile to durable, within the
  stratum).

• synaptic_stratum (four): the stratum, the mossy-fiber synapse bearer, the long-term
  potentiation occurrent, and the plasticity disposition.

• region_stratum (twelve): the stratum, the hippocampus bearer, the separation,
  completion, and recall occurrents, the storage disposition, the cue-input and
  recall-output ports, the region-internal recall causal_relation_object, the recall
  conduit, the cross-stratal consolidation causal_relation_object (skips-true), and
  the Ed25519-signed provenance assertion over it.

Each record is written to structure/<name>.json. Every record identity is a hash of
its canonical content, so the artifacts are reproducible: minting the same content
twice yields the same identity.


PART EIGHT - THE TECHNOLOGY STACK IN ONE VIEW

• Language and runtime: SWI-Prolog, with packs on the library path.
• Coordination: the PrologAI Lattice (stigmergy plus await and notify) and a
  cyclic_actor background thread.
• Structure: Causalontology 2.0.0 records, validated by PrologAI's conformance engine
  and JSON schema validator, signed with pure-Prolog Ed25519.
• Safety: PrologAI's N8 membership contract, declared on recall.
• Orchestration and gates: small bash scripts under bin, plus a Python closure check;
  the mini regression runs through PrologAI's own harness.
• Documentation: ERC comments on every line of Prolog, and this six-file SPARC set.
